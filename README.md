# National Service Scheme (NSS) Management System

## About National Service Scheme (NSS)

The **National Service Scheme (NSS)** is a Government of India initiative that encourages students and youth to participate in community service and social development activities. NSS focuses on developing a sense of responsibility, leadership, teamwork, social awareness, and service among volunteers.

The motto of NSS is:

> **"Not Me, But You"**

It represents the importance of selfless service and putting the needs of society before oneself.

---

## About the Project

The **NSS Management System** is a web-based application designed to digitally manage and organize the activities, events, camps, volunteers, meetings, and reports of an NSS unit.

The application helps maintain complete and organized records of NSS programs, volunteers, events, camps, meetings, attendance, and reports in one place.

---

## Key Features

* **NSS Activity Management**

  * Create and manage NSS activities
  * Maintain activity details and records
  * Track completed and upcoming activities

* **Event Management**

  * Create and manage NSS events
  * Maintain event schedules and details
  * Manage event-related information

* **Camp Management**

  * Manage NSS special camps and other camps
  * Maintain camp details and records
  * Track volunteer participation

* **Volunteer Management**

  * Maintain NSS volunteer records
  * Manage volunteer information
  * Track volunteer participation in activities and events

* **Meeting Management**

  * Schedule and maintain volunteer meetings
  * Store meeting details and records
  * Maintain meeting history

* **Attendance Management**

  * Maintain volunteer attendance
  * Track participation in activities, events, camps, and meetings

* **Report Management**

  * Maintain NSS activity reports
  * Store event and camp reports
  * Organize records for future reference

* **Centralized Management**

  * Manage NSS information from a single platform
  * Reduce manual paperwork
  * Keep records organized and easily accessible

---


## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. To digitally manage NSS activities and events.
2. To maintain organized volunteer records.
3. To manage NSS camps and meetings.
4. To maintain attendance and participation records.
5. To store and manage NSS reports digitally.
6. To reduce manual paperwork and record-keeping.
7. To provide a centralized management system for NSS Program Officers and Coordinators.
8. To make NSS administration more efficient and organized.
